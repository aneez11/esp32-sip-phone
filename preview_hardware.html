Hardware

GPIO wiring for this board. Every value is a GPIO number; use -1 for anything that is not wired. Saving restarts the device.

Wrong pins leave the display or the audio silent. Change one group at a time and check the serial log if a peripheral stops working. Blank fields keep the stored value.

I2S audio

Digital audio link to the microphone and speaker or codec (INMP441 + MAX98357A, ES8388, ...).

Audio towards the speaker/codec.
Audio from the microphone.
Only codecs that need MCLK (e.g. ES8388); otherwise -1.

I2C control

Control bus used by I2C audio codecs (ES8388/ES8311) and I2C keypads or OLEDs.

Display (SPI)

SPI bus for the ST7789, ILI9341 or GC9A01 panel.

Panel data input - usually the SDI/SDA pad of the module.
Most panels do not drive this line; keep -1.
Sometimes labelled RS or A0 on the module.
Reset line of the panel; -1 if tied to EN.

Touch

XPT2046 resistive touch controller (shares the display SPI bus).

Separate chip select for the touch controller.
Pen-down interrupt; optional (-1 polls instead).

Display theme

Look of the on-device screen. The theme is drawn by LVGL and applied after the restart.

Every value is a GPIO number, -1 means not connected. The device restarts so the drivers re-initialise.
