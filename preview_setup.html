Mode & audio

How this device behaves when somebody calls it, and which audio hardware it drives.

Phone rings until somebody answers on the keypad, screen or this page. Speaker picks up by itself: ideal for paging, intercom and doorbell use.
Speaker mode only: 0 answers immediately, up to 30 s gives people time to move away from the speaker.
Plain I2S amp for MAX98357A, PCM5102, UDA1334 and similar DACs that have no control bus (volume is then scaled in software). I2C codec for ES8388/ES8311 modules. Auto picks the codec when I2C pins are wired and it answers, otherwise a plain amp. Changing this restarts the device.
Playback level, currently 80%. 0 mutes the speaker. Applies immediately, no restart.
SIP account and login changes apply immediately. Wi-Fi changes restart the device.
